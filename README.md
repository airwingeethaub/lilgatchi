# lilgatchi

Hello, world! This is lilgatchi, a digital pet project.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a web browser. No installation is needed.

- Your critter gets a little hungrier every 5 minutes. Press **Feed** to keep it full. If its fullness runs out, its health starts dropping instead.
- Press **Medicine** to restore health.
- Press **Train** to build fitness. Training occasionally hurts the critter a little.
- Press **Lights off** to turn off the light. After its 9 PM bedtime, a critter in the dark falls asleep.

The clock on the picture shows the time. Progress is saved in your browser, and time keeps passing while the window is closed.

The **Debug** tab has testing tools: a faster hunger pace, a 2x speed switch, and a way to set the clock by hand.
