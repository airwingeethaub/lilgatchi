# lilgatchi

Hello, world! This is lilgatchi, a digital pet project.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a web browser. No installation is needed. The first time, you choose a Frog, a Clown, or a Dog.

- Your pet gets a little hungrier every 5 minutes. Press **Feed** to keep it full. If its fullness runs out, its health starts dropping instead.
- Press **Medicine** to restore health.
- Press **Train** to build fitness. Training occasionally hurts your pet a little.
- Your pet poops every 30 to 100 minutes. Press **Clean** to clean up. If more than 4 poops pile up, its health suffers.
- Press **Lights off** to turn off the light. After its 9 PM bedtime, a pet in the dark falls asleep.
- Press **Items** to see what your pet owns and use it. Every pet starts with $1,000 and a few things.
- Press **Travel** to visit **The Shop**, where you can spend money, or the **Bathhouse**, which gets your pet clean again.
- **Play** is coming later.

The **Status** tab shows health, fullness, fitness, and cleanliness. The **Attributes** tab shows your pet's species and personality. Progress is saved in your browser, and time keeps passing while the window is closed.

The **Debug** tab has testing tools: a faster hunger pace, a button to make your pet poop right away, a way to set the clock by hand, and a button to start over with a new pet.
